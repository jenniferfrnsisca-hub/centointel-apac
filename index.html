Sales intelligence

Turn evidence into customer conversations

Customer objection

“Our current laboratory already provides WES/WGS.”

Suggested response: Ask what happens when the first analysis is unresolved, whether reanalysis is structured, and how phenotype and longitudinal information are incorporated into interpretation.

Discovery questions

  • What is your current diagnostic yield?
  • How are VUS and unresolved cases handled?
  • Do you have a reanalysis pathway?
  • Which difficult genomic disorders are hardest to diagnose?
  • Is oncology the only genomic testing need?

Positioning principle

Do not compete only on gene count, price or turnaround. Anchor the conversation in clinical question → technology → interpretation → data → report → follow-up.

Confidence discipline

Use “verified,” “inference,” and “unknown.” If a competitor capability cannot be confirmed from a credible source, say so explicitly.
